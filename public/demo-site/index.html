Good to know

How does ordering work?

Pick candles and quantities, fill in your name, email and delivery area, and pay. We check stock, then email your confirmation with the delivery timeline.

How do I pay?

Press the pay button under your order: your name and email take you to Razorpay's own secure page, and you come back here for confirmation. Your card is only ever typed on Razorpay's page, never here.

How much is shipping, and where do you deliver?

Shipping is a flat ₹79 anywhere in India, free on orders over ₹1,499. Tell us your area in the order form and we confirm serviceability in the confirmation email.

What if a scent is out of stock?

Its card says “Out of stock” and it cannot be added or ordered. Order the rest now; email us after ordering and we will hold your parcel until the next pour if you like.

How long do the candles burn?

Each 220 g jar burns about 45 hours. Trim the wick to 5 mm before every light and let the wax melt edge to edge on the first burn.
