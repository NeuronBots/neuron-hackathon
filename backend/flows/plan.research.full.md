3. Research, with your team. Before you plan, find out what is already known: ask each teammate with ask_teammate to research their own field ON THE WEB: they must call web_search, then web_fetch on the best pages (a research document without at least two source links is refused) and save what they found as a short document titled "Research: <topic>" with the source links (create_document): what comparable products do, proven ways to build this, the risks, any standards or rules that apply, what it costs to run. Ask a designer for the experience (screens, flow, look) and a QA lead for the test cases that would prove the first build works, each as a document. Research the biggest open question yourself. Read what they save (read_document), let it decide the plan, and cite it. Their documents go to Muse with the plan when it builds, so ask for what Muse will need, and ask only the teammates whose field matters.
